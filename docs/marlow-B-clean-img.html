01 / The idea

A home that runs itself.

Marlow House is built for people who want the ease of an apartment without giving up the feeling of a house. Wide floor plates, deep balconies and a building team that handles the rest.

Every residence faces either the harbour or the courtyard garden. None face a corridor.

Questions

Good to know.

When is completion?

Construction starts early 2026 with completion scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2027.

What is the deposit?

A 10% deposit on exchange, with the balance due on settlement. Deposits are held in a trust account.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Marlow House is pet friendly, with a wash station in the basement and the courtyard garden steps from the lobby.

Can I see a display suite?

The display suite at 14 Marine Terrace is open Saturdays 10am to 2pm, or by appointment any weekday.
